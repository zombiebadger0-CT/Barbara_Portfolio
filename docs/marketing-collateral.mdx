# Marketing Collateral

## Turning Product Information into Marketing Collateral

**The ask:** When ZAGENO launched its integration program, I was asked to create an email flyer that would encourage customers to connect their procurement software to the ZAGENO Marketplace.

**Process:**

1. **Structure the content.** I moved the source material from an existing online article into a Word document and mapped out how the information should flow. ([First draft](#first-draft))
2. **Tighten and reorganize.** On a second pass, I cut filler and passive phrasing, shortened the headers, combined the Point Purchasing notes into one side note, and grouped the content into scannable sections that end with a clear call to action. ([Second draft](#second-draft))
3. **Design the layout.** I built the final flyer in Canva. The integration steps became a vertical process strip, and the two-week timeline and supported systems went into a right sidebar. ([Final draft](#final-draft))

**Result:** The flyer went out in the integration marketing campaign emails and was used in customer success outreach.
